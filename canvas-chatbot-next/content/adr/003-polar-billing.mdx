# ADR-003: Polar Billing

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2025-02-15

## Context

Lulu offers premium features (e.g., advanced AI models, higher usage) that require a billing system. We needed subscription management, payment processing, and webhook handling for subscription events.

## Decision

Use **Polar.sh** for billing and subscriptions. Polar handles checkout, customer portal, webhooks, and product/price management. We sync subscription status into our `dev.users`, `dev.subscriptions`, and `dev.payments` tables via webhooks.

## Consequences

**Positive:**
- Polar is built for developer tools and SaaS; good fit for our audience
- Webhooks keep our DB in sync with Polar state
- Customer portal for self-service (cancel, update payment)
- Simpler than building on Stripe directly for subscription lifecycle

**Negative:**
- Less mature than Stripe; smaller ecosystem
- Vendor lock-in for subscription logic
- Must handle webhook verification and idempotency ourselves
